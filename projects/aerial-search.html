Where to search next空中多目标搜索

Informative planning for aerial multi-target search and tracking: an attention-based hybrid belief policy decides where to fly so that uncertainty drops fastest.面向无人机多目标搜索与跟踪的信息规划：用注意力混合信念策略决定下一步飞哪儿，让不确定性下降得最快。

DOIDOI IEEE XploreIEEE Xplore
01

Motivation动机

When targets move and are only partially observed, the question is not where they are — it is where to fly next to learn the most. The belief over target states is hybrid: discrete over which target, continuous over where it is.

目标会动、又只能部分观测时，问题不是「它们在哪儿」，而是「下一步飞哪儿信息量最大」。目标状态的信念是混合的：哪个目标（离散）+ 在哪儿（连续）。

02

What we did做了什么

  • Encode the hybrid belief with attention over targets and observations.
  • Learn an informative planning policy with reinforcement learning, rewarding uncertainty reduction per unit of travel.
  • 用注意力机制对目标与观测编码混合信念。
  • 用强化学习训练信息规划策略，奖励「单位航程内的不确定性下降」。

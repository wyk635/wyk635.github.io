I am **Yike Wu (吴逸坷)**, a master's student in Control Science and Engineering at [Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen](https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/). I previously received a B.Eng. in Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering from [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/).

My research focuses on **air-ground collaborative robotics**, **vision-and-language navigation (VLN)**, and **reinforcement learning** for mobile robots. I work on cross-view depth completion, active reconstruction, and long-horizon navigation agents.

I am the first author of an IROS 2026 paper on air-ground collaborative reconstruction via cross-view depth completion. I have also interned at **X Square Robot (自变量机器人)**, Spatial Computing and Mobility Department, where I built a VLN agent and a 3DGS interactive simulation platform, and at the **Meituan Drone Business Department**, where I worked on multi-vehicle planning and air-ground docking.

First-author work accepted at **IROS 2026**. We propose **RefineNet**, which fuses monocular UAV images with sparse UGV LiDAR to recover metric, dense aerial depth for large-scale reconstruction.

A three-stage network with per-stage confidence, a dynamic GNN, and sparse attention propagates sparse ground measurements across viewpoint changes and occlusions.

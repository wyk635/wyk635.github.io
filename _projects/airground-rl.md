Ongoing project (2026.01–present) on **air-ground collaborative active reconstruction**.

I design a topology-aware sampling strategy to improve expert viewpoint trajectories, then train a policy with imitation learning followed by offline reinforcement learning. The resulting policy can outperform the expert planner on reconstruction quality.

About关于

Perception → decision感知 → 决策

I am Yike Wu (吴逸坷), a master's student in Control Science and Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, and before that an undergraduate in Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering at South China University of Technology. 我是吴逸坷，现就读于 哈尔滨工业大学（深圳）控制科学与工程专业， 本科毕业于 华南理工大学智能制造工程专业。

My work sits between perception and decision making: cross-view depth completion that gives a monocular aerial image metric scale from ground LiDAR, active reconstruction policies for large-scene 3D/2DGS learned from expert planners, and long-horizon vision-and-language navigation agents. The interesting part is never the benchmark average — it is what the system does when the map is wrong, the view is oblique and the horizon is long. 我的工作处在感知与决策之间：用地面 LiDAR 为单目航拍图像恢复度量尺度的 跨视角深度补全；面向大场景 3D/2DGS 重建、从专家规划器里学出来的 主动重建策略；以及长时程的视觉语言导航智能体。 真正有意思的从来不是平均指标，而是当地图是错的、视角是斜的、时程很长时， 系统还能不能跑下去。
